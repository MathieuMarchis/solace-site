06
Our Solution

Solace turns a 10-second selfie into a routine built for one face — yours.

A user scans, gets an honest read on his skin, and receives a specific AM and PM routine within a minute of opening the app. Because Solace sells no products, the routine is the only thing we have to be right about. Every re-scan is stored, so the app gets more useful the longer he stays.

Cosmetic personalization only. Solace does not diagnose or treat any condition.

07

A specific AM and PM routine — not a product list and not a score

Ordered steps he can tick off morning and night, each one tied to a finding from his scan. Product suggestions link out to Sephora, Amazon and Ulta because Solace has no shelf to protect — the only incentive is getting it right.

08
Our Solution

The retention engine: a training log for your face

Every scan is stored, dated and comparable — so progress becomes visible

A score you check once is a novelty. A log you build over twelve weeks is a habit. Each entry keeps the date and the concerns detected at that moment, so a user can watch "Excess Oil · Moderate" become "Excess Oil · Mild" instead of guessing whether anything changed.

This is the mechanic that turns a first-week subscriber into a twelve-month one — and it is the thing a brand-owned scan tool structurally cannot build, because it only ever sees one scan at the point of sale.

Dated

Compared
Severity tracked scan over scan
Owned
A dataset that compounds with use

Why Now

Three curves crossed in the last twenty-four months — and they only cross once

01
Young men started buying skincare out loud
Male skincare is growing at 6.5% a year, faster than the category overall, and the 19–35 band already dominates spend. The cultural permission problem that blocked this market for thirty years is gone.
02
Vision models got good enough to run on a phone photo
Reading visible skin traits from a single uncontrolled selfie was a lab problem in 2020 and is an API call in 2026. The moat moved from the model to the routine logic and the retention loop on top of it.
03
Distribution is free again — briefly
Short-form video still hands unpaid reach to a founder who posts daily. Solace's first eight subscribers cost $0 to acquire. That window closes as this category gets crowded.
